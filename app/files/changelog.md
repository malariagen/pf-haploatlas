## Version Pf9.0.0 - February 2026
- Ingested the MalariaGEN Pf9 data release - now 40,074 QC-passed samples. Alongside samples sequenced at the Sanger Institute, Pf9 now includes ENA samples sequenced by other organisations. Sample collection may have been biased at the following locations and collection years: Brazil (Roraima: 2022–2023); Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa: 2017; Sud-Kivu: 2017); Ethiopia (Amhara: 2017; Tigray: 2017–2018); Tanzania (Kagera: 2021–2022); Uganda (Apac: 2020; Busia: 2018–2019; Katakwi: 2019–2020; Kitgum: 2017, 2019–2020; Kotido: 2019–2020; Mbale: 2021–2022; Pader: 2017, 2019–2022). Haplotype prevalence trends from these locations should be interpreted with caution. 
- Previous Pf-HaploAtlas Pf8 version has been made available [here](https://pf8-haploatlas.streamlit.app/) (it may require waking up the app!).
